# KING OF XAU/NAS — Signal Terminal

## Render Environment Variables

Required:
- TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN
- TWELVE_DATA_API_KEY

Recommended:
- TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID

Optional:
- XAU_SYMBOL=XAU/USD
- NAS_SYMBOL=NDX
- ENTRY_INTERVAL=5min
- MAIN_INTERVAL=15min
- HTF_INTERVAL=1h
- SCAN_SECONDS=60
- MIN_CONFIDENCE=82
- SIGNAL_COOLDOWN_SECONDS=1800

## Important

This bot sends only qualifying signals. It does not send WAIT messages.

If Twelve Data uses a different NAS100 symbol for your plan, change `NAS_SYMBOL` in Render without changing the code.

The `/health` command lets you diagnose the scanner from Telegram.

For true 24/7 operation on Render, use an always-on service/plan or an appropriate external uptime strategy. A free service may sleep depending on Render's current service rules.
